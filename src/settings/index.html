Taskbar badge
Show the total unread count on the Windows taskbar icon.
Prefetch background accounts
Load your other accounts right after launch, for instant switching. Uses more memory. When off, an account loads the first time you click it; unread counts and notifications work either way. Applies on next launch.
Auto-sleep background Gmail accounts
Unload Gmail accounts you haven’t used for 10 minutes to free memory. They keep checking for new mail; clicking one reloads it. Other services can only be checked while open, so they stay loaded.
Start at login
Launch Minimail when you sign in to Windows.
