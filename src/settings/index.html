Taskbar badge
Show the total unread count on the Windows taskbar icon.
Prefetch background accounts
Load your other accounts right after launch for instant switching. Uses more memory. Gmail can check for new mail before you open the account. Applies on next launch.
Auto-sleep background Gmail accounts
Unload Gmail accounts you haven’t used for 10 minutes to free memory. They keep checking for new mail; clicking one reloads it. Other services can only be checked while open, so they stay loaded unless the switch below is on.
Also unload Proton, Outlook, Yahoo and AOL accounts
They can only be checked while open, so instead of staying loaded they reload out of sight every 5 minutes to look for new mail. Their notifications can then be up to 5 minutes late, and they reload when you click them.
Also unload the account on screen when I’m away
Once the window has been minimized or fully covered for 10 minutes, unload the Gmail account it is showing as well. New mail is still notified; it reloads when you come back. Never while you have a compose open.
Start at login
Launch Minimail when you sign in to Windows.
